About Connected Conservation

Drawing on decades of field research and experience, Connected Conservation develops new ways to resolve human-wildlife conflict in Southern Africa. Multidisciplinary scientific research is combined with community-based strategies to understand conflict situations and provide practical solutions.

Our work includes pioneering community-based conflict mitigation, such as using chilli as a wildlife deterrent, satellite tracking of elephants, technical advice and training, and contributing to the Kavango Zambezi Transfrontier Conservation Area.

What is agro-ecology?

Agro-ecology is a way of farming that works with nature instead of against it. It applies ecological principles to crops, soil, water and local knowledge so that farms stay productive while the surrounding environment stays healthy.

  • Facts about agro-ecology:
  • Protects crops without harming wildlife
  • Uses natural deterrents such as chilli
  • Builds household income and food security
  • Keeps farming and conservation working together
